# P2-0035 — Restore book stem figures: skupina IV

## Scope confirmed

`book_overrides.json` "page" is a PDF page index (1-based); item's "tiskane str. 322-329"
is that same range printed 5 higher (pdf 317-324). Ran `fig_audit.py` on the pre-change
tree and filtered its candidate table to `bk-teh-4-*` with `figure: NO`, which gave 10 ids
(`-10, -13, -14, -15, -16, -17, -19, -43, -46, -47`) plus `bk-teh-4-48` (already in
`book_overrides.json` as `stacked-options`, needing only a `figure` key, per the item) —
11 outstanding, matching the item's count exactly. `bk-teh-4-37` (also a pre-existing
`stacked-options` entry on this page range) was checked and confirmed NOT to need a
figure — its stem/options are pure text about NF-amplifier staging, no picture on the
page; the item's own wording ("Three ... already have one, so 11 are outstanding") is
consistent with excluding it.

## What was found, and the lesson from 0033 applied

Every one of the 11 ids was read from `book_parsed2.json` and cross-checked against the
**rendered PDF page** (pymupdf, 200-400dpi), not taken on faith from `fig_windows.json`'s
own proposals (this run's own calibration printout shows 6 of 10 measurable `bk-teh-4-*`
proposals missing the ~10pt usability bar by 34-262pt). Windows were measured by hand from
`page.get_text("dict")` line boundaries and confirmed by rendering each candidate crop
standalone before writing it to the override, then re-inspected from the actual
post-`postprocess()` PNG in `figs_book/` after the real pipeline run.

Checked by eye for the same class of defect that blocked 0033 (a stem/options block
actually belonging to a different question). Found one, on this page range:

- **`bk-teh-4-47`** — its coordinate-parsed options were **`bk-teh-4-48`'s** text options
  ("10% moduliran; 50% moduliran; preko 100%...") verbatim. Rendering page 324 (pdf; folio
  329) confirmed 47's real stem ("Za posredno mjerenje otpora služimo se shemom spajanja
  kao u:") is followed by **four circuit diagrams labelled a)/b)/c)/d)** (ammeter/voltmeter
  arrangements for indirect resistance measurement) — a figure-options question, same
  species as `bk-teh-4-07`/`-36`. Because 48 has no OCR'd anchor number, the parser's
  next-text-block heuristic handed 48's own (correct) options to 47. Fixed by adding a new
  `figure-options` override: `question` unchanged from print, `options` set to the
  placeholder `a)/b)/c)/d)` (matching the established figure-options convention), `figure`
  window `[287, 515]` covering all four diagrams, `figure_is_options: true`. This does not
  touch the answer letter (the override never sets `answer`; that still comes from
  `book_keys.json` by position) — confirmed `book_parsed2.json`'s own `answer: "a"` for
  this id is untouched by the patch mechanism in `book_figures.py`.

Two more stem defects, not flagged by name in the item but found by rendering:

- **`bk-teh-4-13`** — has **no printed prose stem at all**; the entire "question" is a
  circuit diagram (quarter-wave transformer, labels `Zul`, `Z0=50Ω`, `Zizl=50Ω`, `λ/4`)
  whose own labels the parser stitched into a garbled stem (`"Z, — Z=som |"`). Restored the
  question text as the diagram's own printed labels, `"Zul"` marked as the sought unknown
  with `= ?` appended (no separate value given for it, unlike the other three) — same
  convention already used by `bk-teh-1-13` (`"...I5 = ?"`) for an identical no-prose,
  labels-only diagram. This is a judgment call, flagged per the brief's rule on citing
  nothing rather than inventing: nothing invented beyond formatting the diagram's own
  labels into a line, and it is the minimum needed to replace a stem that currently ships
  as unreadable OCR noise. Options were left exactly as already parsed (`"25Q"`, `"75Q"`,
  `"500"`, `"150.Q"`) — the Ω→Q/0 glyph substitution is a pre-existing, book-wide OCR
  artifact unrelated to this figure and out of this item's `files:` scope (would need
  `parse_book2.py`); noted here rather than fixed.
- **`bk-teh-4-16`** — parsed stem truncated to `"Sprega između krugova A i"`, dropping
  `" B je:"`. The diagram's own `A`/`B` terminal labels sit inline with where those words
  print, and the coordinate parser lost the tail after the picture broke the line run.
  Rendered the page and restored the full sentence `"Sprega između krugova A i B je:"`;
  options (`kapacitivna` / `induktivna-magnetska` / `elektrostatska` / `otpornička`)
  already matched the page and were left untouched.

The other 8 ids (`-10, -14, -15, -17, -19, -43, -46`, plus `-48`'s added figure key) needed
only a crop window — stem and options were already correct (`figure-plain` /
`stacked-options`), confirmed by direct comparison against the rendered page.

In no case was an answer letter touched. `figure_is_options` is `true` only for
`bk-teh-4-47` (its options genuinely are diagrams) and stays `false` for everything else
added here, per the item's Avoid. The three pre-existing figures (`bk-teh-4-07`, `-08`,
`-36`) were not modified — confirmed by diff, only new keys/entries were added.
