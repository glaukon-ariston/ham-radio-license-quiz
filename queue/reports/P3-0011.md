# P3-0011 — Book notes, skupina II tehnički (b)

## Scope

Wrote `quiz/build/notes_book_s2_tehnicki_b.json`: one teaching note per id in
`quiz/build/work/packet_book_s2_tehnicki_b.json` (30 ids, `bk-teh-2-02` through `bk-teh-2-60`,
even numbers only — the complement of P3-0010's odd-numbered `_a` packet). Same simple
`{note, cite, formula?}` shape as the other `notes_book_*` files — no `links` key, since
`link_check.py`/`check_notes.py` only glob `notes_zzz_expanded_*`, so this file adds no links at
all rather than adding unverifiable ones. `packet_book_s2_tehnicki_b.json` (also listed in
`files:`) was read only, not edited.

## What was done

- Read the packet, then compared every id's `question`/`options`/`answer` against the current
  live build (`quiz/questions.json` after `rebuild.py`) — 29/30 matched exactly, one
  (`bk-teh-2-16`) differs only by a trailing stray OCR `" a"` on the question text, not the
  options or answer.
- Rendered every printed page this packet's 30 questions live on (298-308 of
  `Radiokomunikacije_ocr.pdf`) with `booktool.py`/PyMuPDF as **pixel images**, not just OCR text,
  and read the actual page images for every stem — including the 8 that carry a figure
  (`bk-teh-2-02`, `-04`, `-06`, `-08`, `-12`, `-16`, `-20`, `-46`, `-58`). Unlike P3-0010's `_a`
  packet, which found three questions with scrambled option text/letters, **all 30 of this
  packet's questions match the printed page exactly** — same option text, same option order, same
  letter under the HRS-marked answer. No transcription defects found or flagged in this packet.
- Verified every citation against the book's own prose or figures, not invented section numbers.
  For the large majority I found the *exact sentence, formula, or figure* the question is drawn
  from — e.g. `bk-teh-2-04`'s ratio-detector schematic (two opposite-polarity diodes on a
  centre-tapped secondary) matches 3.5.3's own circuit description and figure verbatim (str. 122);
  `bk-teh-2-16`'s 9-resistor grid reduces by symmetry to exactly 1 kΩ, confirmed against the
  rendered figure, not just trusted from the answer key (str. 99 for the series/parallel
  formulas, str. 301 for the figure); `bk-teh-2-36`'s "amplitudna modulacija u izlaznom stupnju"
  is a near-verbatim match to 5.1.1's "modulator treba priključiti na posljednji stupanj
  odašiljača" (str. 136); `bk-teh-2-58`'s N1/N2/N3 filter points match the book's own annotated
  block diagram and its N1/N2/N3 notes word for word (str. 191). For **one id**, `bk-teh-2-50`
  (corroded antenna joint causing mixing/rectification products, the "rusty bolt" effect), I
  could **not** find this specific phenomenon anywhere in the book's continuous prose — searched
  for "korodiran", "hrđa", "labav", "oksid", "nelinearni kontakt" and related terms, and read the
  general EMC/intermodulation sections (str. 188-189, 196-198) by hand; none name corrosion as a
  nonlinear-junction source. Per the "cite nothing rather than something plausible" rule, that
  note says explicitly, in the note text itself, that the answer is taken from the HRS answer
  table, not derived from the book's own text, and the citation points at the answer table plus
  the nearest (but not identical) general topic rather than inventing a matching passage.
- Rendered pixmaps (not text) for pages 298, 299, 300, 301, 302, 306, 308 specifically to check
  circuit topology by eye for `bk-teh-2-02` (transformer 1:4, 32 kΩ on the secondary — confirmed
  the reflected-impedance arithmetic against the actual figure, 32000×(1/4)²=2000 Ω), `bk-teh-2-06`
  (confirmed it's a single-transistor AF stage driving a loudspeaker through transformer T, not a
  generic amplifier guess), `bk-teh-2-08` (confirmed Colpitts-style capacitive divider C3/C4 in
  the tank), `bk-teh-2-12` (confirmed the 50 Ω ∥ (25 Ω+25 Ω) topology behind the 200 mA answer),
  `bk-teh-2-16` (confirmed the 3×3 resistor grid), `bk-teh-2-20` (confirmed which of the four
  filter sketches — d — matches the book's own multi-element mains-filter construction, sl.
  9.2.8, as opposed to the two simple two-element LC sketches or the resistive one), and
  `bk-teh-2-46` (confirmed the three stylised trapezoid icons get progressively more pointed,
  matching the book's own "50% / 100% / preko 100%" trapezoid figure 8.1.7, with the sharpest one
  = answer c).
- Ran a script that re-parses every `cite.loc` in the new file, extracts every "tiskana str. N"
  page number (39 distinct pages across ranges), and confirms each one resolves to a real PDF
  page via `booktool.load('RK')`'s folio map — all resolve.
