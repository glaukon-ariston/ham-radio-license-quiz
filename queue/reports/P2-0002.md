`parse_book2.py` was listed in the item's `files:` but not touched. It is a coordinate parser
for the PDF; a hand classification is editorial data and does not belong inside it, and
putting it there would have made every future re-run of the parser a place where the
classification could be silently lost. `book_subsections.json` sits next to the two data
files the book parser already reads (`book_keys.json`, `book_overrides.json`).

## The vocabulary

`parse2.py` reads the ten technical-syllabus headings off the HRS technical bank's own pages
and files each HRS technical question under one:

    1. Električna, elektromagnetska i radijska teorija     6. Antene i antenski vodovi
    2. Komponente                                          7. Rasprostiranje elektromagnetskog vala
    3. Krugovi                                             8. Mjerenja
    4. Prijamnici                                          9. Smetnje i imunitet
    5. Odašiljači (predajnici)                            10. Električna sigurnost

The generator takes those ten strings **verbatim out of `bank.json`** rather than retyping
them, so the book cannot end up with a string that differs by a space and forms a second,
invisible vocabulary. `rebuild.py` now re-checks that at build time anyway.

## The 360 that cannot be classified

The item says every `questions_extra` entry should end up with a non-null subsection drawn
from the existing HRS vocabulary. **For propisi and pravila there is no such vocabulary.**
Checked, not assumed:

- `parse2.py` only looks for chapter headings when `section == "tehnicki"`. All 95 HRS
  propisi and all 79 HRS pravila questions carry `subsection: None`.
- I then went to the two source PDFs directly and looked for headings the parser might be
  throwing away. `RA_ispiti_7_..._Pravila_i_postupci.pdf` has **none at all** — the only
  all-caps line in the document is `„ A „ RAZRED` on the title page.
- `RA_ispiti_8_..._propisi.pdf` *does* carry 21 unnumbered topical headings (`POJMOVI`,
  `POZIVNE OZNAKE`, `DNEVNIK AMATERSKE POSTAJE`, `INSPEKCIJSKI NADZOR`, …). That is a real
  vocabulary, but it is not an *existing* one: no question in the corpus uses it today, and
  minting it for the 120 book propisi questions while the 95 HRS ones stay null would create
  exactly the split the item is trying to close. Extracting it would mean changing
  `parse2.py` (not in this item's `files:`) and re-subsectioning HRS questions whose packets
  are already finished. **Named here and left alone** — it is a clean separate item if
  Glaukon wants propisi cut by topic.

So: 359 of the 360 technical book questions classified; 240 pravila and 120 propisi left
`None`, matching their HRS counterparts exactly. `make_packets.py --book` still cuts those
by section, the same way the HRS ones are cut.

## How the 359 were classified

Read the stem of every one of the 360, against the HRS bank's own boundaries (which I dumped
by subsection first, precisely to calibrate — HRS's boundaries are not the obvious ones).
The four rules that decided the boundary cases, all recorded in the JSON's `_rules`:

1. **Modulation theory → 3, transmitter stages → 5.** HRS files *every* AM/FM/SSB/CW
   question under `3. Krugovi`, including modulation index, spectra and the trapezoid
   oscillogram. `5. Odašiljači` is for the block chain: buffer, driver, multiplier, Pi
   network, limiter, PA efficiency, where in the chain the modulator sits.
2. **Harmonic arithmetic → 3, a harmonic landing in someone else's band → 9.** HRS puts
   "Drugi parni harmonik od 1750 kHz je:" in 3 and "Peti harmonik … upada u područje:" in 9.
3. **Chained questions inherit their anchor.** Eleven questions refer to another by number
   ("Zavojnica iz zadatka 47", "kao u pitanju 19", "Signal prikazan u zadatku 42"). These
   take the anchor's subsection so the chain lands in one packet — splitting a chain would
   hand a worker half a question.
4. **Bare figure captions** ("Sklop na slici je:", "Na slici je:") are classified from the
   subject matter of all four options, never from which one is correct. Where the options
   are receiver detectors the question is 4; where they are oscillators, 3; where they are
   interference filters, 9.

### The independent check

57 book stems match an HRS stem exactly. 19 of those matches are worthless — they are short
generic captions ("Sklop na slici je:") that collide with unrelated HRS figures, so the
generator only counts a twin when the stem is ≥ 40 characters. That leaves **38 real twins,
where HRS has already ruled on the topic. All 38 agreed with the hand classification** —
zero disagreements. That is a check on the boundaries above, not a restatement of them: the
classification was written from the stems before the twin comparison ran.

Where a twin exists the generator uses HRS's value regardless, so HRS remains the authority.

### Distribution

    1. teorija    16     4. prijamnici  38     7. rasprostiranje 47    10. sigurnost 19
    2. komponente 24     5. odašiljači  25     8. mjerenja       33
    3. krugovi    80     6. antene      18     9. smetnje        59

Against HRS's own shape (35/29/74/18/14/33/41/15/15/8 over 282) the mock exams lean much
harder on interference (59 vs an expected ~19) and measurements, and much lighter on
antennas — skupina V contains no antenna-theory question at all. That is the priručnik's
weighting, not a classification artefact; the 21 questions in the 49–60 slot of most papers
are an EMC run.

### The one left unset

`bk-teh-4-13`. Its stem survives OCR only as `Z, — Z=som |` — a bare figure with four
impedance options and no readable text. Its neighbours are antenna questions, which is
exactly why guessing 6 would have been wrong to do: that is inference from position, not
from the question. Left `None` and recorded in the JSON's `_unset` with the reason.
`make_packets.py` reports it as unassigned rather than dropping it silently.

## Found and left alone

**Four book questions carry their neighbour's option block.** `bk-teh-1-47`, `bk-teh-2-03`,
`bk-teh-5-55` are exact copies of the options of question *n+1*; `bk-teh-4-47` is the same
with two OCR slips. All four are figure questions whose real options are pictures or a
diagram list, and the parser has taken the following question's column instead.

This matters more than it looks: `rebuild.py`'s integrity check only asks whether the answer
*letter* exists among the options, so these pass while pointing at the wrong text. The book's
key table row for question 47 marks a letter that is being applied to question 48's options.
`bk-teh-1-47`'s stem matches an HRS question exactly, so its correct answer is independently
known and could be used to confirm the damage.

Not touched — it is an answer-integrity problem in `parse_book2.py`/`book_overrides.json`,
not a classification one, and fixing it inside this item would have been scope creep. Worth
its own P2: **four book questions show the wrong options**, remedy is four
`book_overrides.json` entries read off the rendered page by eye.

(The three-way group `bk-teh-4-07/08/36` also share an option block, but legitimately — all
three are "a) b) c) d)" figure-choice questions.)
