## What was done, and the P2-0033 lesson applied

Per the orchestrator's explicit warning about P2-0033 (skupina II blocked/failed: worker
declared done without re-running `fig_audit.py`, and one id had an unrelated merged-block
OCR defect needing figure-options treatment, not a plain crop), every one of the 19 ids was:

1. Read from `book_parsed2.json` and cross-checked against the **rendered PDF page**
   (pymupdf, 150–200dpi), not taken on faith from `fig_audit.py`'s own `fig_windows.json`
   proposals — those are explicitly unvalidated outside the ground-truth set (0031's own
   report), and this batch confirmed that again: 9 of the 17 proposals missed the
   ~10pt usability bar by 11–466pt (see `fig_audit.py`'s own calibration printout after the
   change), mostly from the "next-anchor" and "option-bottom+5" fallback branches guessing
   wrong when option text is short (numeric answers) or partially non-text.
2. Checked by eye for the same class of defect that broke 0033: a stem/options block that
   actually belongs to a DIFFERENT question. None found — every stem+options set matches
   its own printed question when read against the rendered page. Two related but distinct
   defects WERE found and handled (below): a genuinely truncated own-stem, and two
   cross-references disguised as GAP/NAME hits.
3. Crop window measured by hand from `page.get_text("dict")` line boundaries (stem's own
   last line -> options' first line) and confirmed visually by rendering the candidate crop
   standalone before writing it to the override.

Added to `book_overrides.json`:

- **`bk-teh-3-06`, `-10`, `-16`, `-51`, `-53`** — `reason: "figure-in-stem"`, question
  patched. These are the item's own named debris examples (`"3`, `HI no`, `—`,
  `stHHIr&=e`) plus `bk-teh-3-51` (also trailing `—`) — all a figure caption glued onto the
  stem by the column-stitching parser, same bug as `bk-teh-5-60`/skupina I. Options
  unchanged, copied verbatim from the parser's own (correct) reading.
- **`bk-teh-3-21`** — also `figure-in-stem`, **not named in the item** but found the same
  way: `book_parsed2.json`'s stored stem ended in a stray trailing `" l"` (a tick-mark glyph
  from the coupling-curve chart's frequency axis) that isn't part of the printed question.
  Patched to drop it; options unchanged.
- **`bk-teh-3-01`** — new reason `"stem-split-column"` (added to `_reasons`, distinct from
  `figure-in-stem`): the printed stem is laid out in **two side-by-side columns at the same
  y** (`"C1=2,0nF; C2=1,5nF; C3=100pF;"` and `"L1=2mH. Ukupni kapacitet iznosi:"`), and the
  coordinate parser kept only the first column, silently dropping the second — not a figure
  bleeding debris IN, but the stem's own text being incomplete. Rendered the page and
  confirmed visually the full sentence is one continuous stem above the capacitor-network
  figure. Restored the missing half verbatim from the page's own OCR text; options untouched.
- **`bk-teh-3-03`, `-09`, `-18`, `-19`, `-20`, `-42`** — `reason: "figure-plain"`, only a
  crop window (stem and options already parsed correctly).
- **`bk-teh-3-05`, `-44`(existing), `-47`** — figure sits **beside** the options at the same
  y (like skupina I's `bk-teh-1-04`), so each uses `figure_x` to exclude the option-text
  column: `-05`'s Z-vs-f curve (x=[215,350]), `-44`'s bridge-measurement circuit
  (x=[30,265]), `-47`'s notch-filter schematic (x=[195,445]). All three confirmed by
  rendering the narrowed crop standalone and checking no option text leaked in.
- **`bk-teh-3-43`, `-54`** — `reason: "cross-reference"` (the existing category, precedent
  `bk-teh-4-08`), **not caught by inspection alone but by the GAP number**: both have a
  GAP-detector hit under 26–65pt (far below a real figure's typical 100–290pt gap in this
  batch) and their stems literally say "signal shown in problem 42" / "filter as in problem
  53" — rendering confirmed both share their referenced question's own figure rather than
  having one of their own. Each entry copies the referenced id's finalised `figure` window
  (`3-43` = `3-42`'s `[153,302]`; `3-54` = `3-53`'s `[170,302]`) and carries no `question`
  key since the parsed stem text is already complete and correct.
- **`bk-teh-3-44`, `bk-teh-3-45`** (pre-existing entries, per the item) — only a `figure`
  key added, `reason`/`question`/`options` untouched. `-45`'s own stem
  ("Blok-shema prikazuje:") is **not present in the PDF's OCR text layer at all** (confirmed
  by dumping raw page text — it simply isn't there, unlike the other five debris cases where
  the text exists but glued wrong); the crop boundary was found by ink-density banding
  (`get_pixmap` on narrow horizontal strips) rather than `find_stem`'s text-matching, since
  there was no OCR text to match against.

In no case was an option or the answer key touched. `figure_is_options` is `false`
throughout, per the item's Avoid. `bk-pra-3-04` (the known false positive) was left alone.
