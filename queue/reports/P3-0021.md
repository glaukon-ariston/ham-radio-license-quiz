## Method

Read the full text of NN150/22 (all 20 PDF pages / articles 1.-26., since the item warns
that partial reads on this series have caused wrong-article citations before), RK's relevant
chapters (3.2, 3.2.1, 3.3.1, 3.3.3, 3.3.4, 3.3.8.1, 3.3.9, 3.3.10, 3.3.11, 3.3.16.2/.3, 1.4,
4.1 — all via `booktool.py page`), PZM str. 56 (digipiteri), and ERC32 (the P-razred exam
syllabus NN150/22 čl. 7 st. 2 points to) with PyMuPDF. Every numeric/age/frequency/fine claim
in every note was pulled and read directly from the source before being written, never reused
from the packet's own `current_cite` (which for all 20 ids is only the answer-key table
location) or its `hrs_source` page numbers.

## Other finding, not fixed (out of `files:`): bk-pro-4-05 option (c) is truncated

The packet's own `options` for bk-pro-4-05 has (a) and (c) identical: both
"9A RS i 2 slova". The printed page (367) actually reads (c) "9A RS i 2 slova i 1 brojke" —
an OCR/transcription clip that dropped the rest of the sentence. The HRS-marked answer is (b),
unaffected, so no flag was needed; only noted in the file's `_note` header and here.

## Verified matches to current law (no flag) vs. confirmed divergence (flag: check)

Of the 20, 7 carry `flag: "check"`:

- **bk-pro-4-02, -09, -14**: inspection-service ban authority / fine amounts trace to the old
  Zakon o telekomunikacijama (RK 3.2/3.2.1); NN150/22 (read in full, čl. 1-26) has no
  equivalent provision, and ZEK (NN 76/22, not in `docs/`) couldn't be checked, so no article
  is cited for the modern basis (rule 5).
- **bk-pro-4-04, -12**: RK's descriptive text gives fixed ages (14 for an A-class licence, 12
  to sit any exam) that NN150/22 no longer states explicitly — the current Pravilnik's only
  age rule is parental consent under 18 for the exam application (čl. 9 st. 2).
- **bk-pro-4-13**: RK's "potvrda o tehničkoj ispravnosti" (samogradnja) is the same
  requirement as NN150/22 čl. 12 st. 3's "uvjerenje o tehničkoj sukladnosti", but not the same
  term — same pattern as `bk-pro-3-15` in the prior packet.
- **bk-pro-4-19**: RK's own "5 godina" licence-validity text has no matching duration figure
  in current NN150/22 (which only requires the licence to *state* a validity period) — same
  pattern as `bk-pro-3-20`.

Direct, word-for-word confirmations against the still-current NN150/22 (no flag): **-03**
(dnevnik retention, 12 mjeseci = 1 godina, čl. 20 st. 3, matches RK's "jednu godinu" too),
**-05** (9A RS + serial number, čl. 15 st. 4 verbatim), **-06** (135,7-137,8 kHz is A-only in
Dodatak 1 Tablica 1 — confirmed absent from the P-razred band list), **-07** (secret codes
excluded by the čl. 17 "obični jezik" requirement), **-08** (ITU Region 2 = Americas, so
Canada, RK 1.4), **-11** (P-razred exam's three components trace through NN150/22 čl. 7 st. 2
to ERC Report 32 §3.2, which literally lists technical/operating/regulatory as the three
mandatory parts), **-15/-16/-17/-18/-20** (each traced to an exact NN150/22 article: čl. 18
t.7, čl. 17, čl. 12 st. 11 t.4, čl. 16 st. 1 + the Pravilnik's own enactment clause, čl. 19
st. 1).

**bk-pro-4-01** (message types) and **bk-pro-4-10** (digipiteri interconnected) were solved by
elimination/PZM rather than a single quoted sentence — both explained honestly in-note,
including that no explicit source could be found banning "religious content" specifically for
-01 (cited nothing for that one sub-claim, per the Avoid clause), and that RK's "no voice link
between two repeater stations" rule (str. 245) is about voice repeaters, a different
technology from digipeaters' packet-forwarding chain (PZM str. 56, confirms up to 8
digipeaters in series) — not a contradiction.
