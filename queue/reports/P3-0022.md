# P3-0022 — Book notes, skupina V tehnički, paket _a

## Scope

Wrote `quiz/build/notes_book_s5_tehnicki_a.json`: one teaching note per id in
`quiz/build/work/packet_book_s5_tehnicki_a.json` (30 ids, `bk-teh-5-01` through `bk-teh-5-59`,
odd numbers only — the `_a` half of skupina V's technical exam, per P2-0005's interleave).
Same `{note, formula?, cite}` shape as the sibling `notes_book_s4_tehnicki_a.json`. No `links`
key — matches the established convention that `link_check.py`/`check_notes.py` only glob
`notes_zzz_expanded_*`. `packet_book_s5_tehnicki_a.json` (also listed in `files:`) was read
only, not edited.

## Method

Dumped RK printed pages 332-343 (the whole SKUPINA V exam paper) with `booktool.py page` for
text, then rendered the same PDF pages (pdf index 326-337, `pymupdf` `get_pixmap` at 3x matrix)
to PNG and viewed every one, because — as the earlier P3 items and the brief both warn — this
PDF's line art is near-invisible to `get_images()`/`get_drawings()` and the OCR text layer
mangles both circuit labels and Greek letters. Every one of the 30 questions' options was
checked word-for-word against the rendered image, not the packet's OCR'd `question`/`options`
text or its `current_cite`/`current_explanation`. Supporting theory citations (formulas,
definitions) were pulled by locating the matching RK chapter via `booktool.py toc`/`find` and
reading the actual page text, e.g. p.108 for the resonant-frequency formula, p.117 for the
bridge-rectifier PIV rule, p.168 for the critical-frequency definition, p.207 Table 10.3 for
the RF-field safety example.

## Major defect: bk-teh-5-55's packet text is question 56's, not question 55's

Rendering printed page 342 showed that the packet's `question` and all four `options` for
`bk-teh-5-55` are actually question 56's ("RF smetnje iz gradske mreže... swap phase/replace
socket/RF filter/thicker cable"). The real question 55 is entirely different and has no text
options at all — it's a figure showing four filter frequency-response curves (a-d), asking
which one would successfully limit harmonic radiation for a transmitter working across the
whole HF band. This is an OCR/parser page-merge, not a one-word typo. The note was written from
the real, page-verified content (curve (a), a low-pass filter with a ~30 MHz corner, is
correct — it passes the whole HF ham range while attenuating everything above it), and the
defect is spelled out both in the file's `_note` header and in the question's own note, per the
"never restate broken text as fact" instruction. Not fixed — outside this item's `files:`.

## OCR-glue defects that do not change the correct answer (3 of 30)

- **bk-teh-5-05**: packet's question text has `R=50Ω2`; the real page says `R = 5 Ω`. Doesn't
  matter — resonant frequency f0=1/(2π√(LC)) doesn't depend on R. Packet's options `5/7 MHz`,
  `1/7 MHz`, `277 MHz` are OCR misreadings of `5/π MHz`, `1/π MHz`, `2π MHz` — confirmed by
  computing f0=5/π MHz≈1.59 MHz exactly, matching the HRS-marked answer (a).
  and 30 π/45 π/60 π/66 π; independently verified Q=2πfL/R=30π matches the HRS answer.
- **bk-teh-5-17**: same π→letter OCR corruption in the options (packet: `30 ze`/`45 z`/`60
  m`/`66 z`); real page reads `30π`/`45π`/`60π`/`66π`. Q=2πfL/R=30π confirmed independently.
- **bk-teh-5-03**: packet options (b)-(d) lost the "V" unit suffix during parsing (`18`/`24`/
  `36` vs the real `18 V`/`24 V`/`36 V`). Cosmetic only.
