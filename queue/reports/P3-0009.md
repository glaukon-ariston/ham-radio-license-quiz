## Defects found and repaired

**1. `bk-pro-1-16` — the defect the item named.** Confirmed exactly as described. NN150/22
Dodatak 1 t. 2.4.3 is titled "Amaterski radiofar" and sets only beacon ERP (50 / 10 / 1 W);
t. 2.4.2, which does cover repeaters and digipeaters, names no frequency band at all. The band
list is RK's own, printed str. 245: "Amaterska relejna postaja može raditi u frekvencijskim
pojasima 144…146 MHz, 430…440 MHz i 1 240…1 300 MHz na posebnim frekvencijskim kanalima."
The paragraph now attributes the list to RK str. 245, says explicitly that the Pravilnik does
*not* carry it (and that čl. 14. st. 5. instead ties a repeater to the frequencies written into
its own licence), and a second `RK` cite was added for the sentence. The first paragraph —
144–146 MHz in both Tablica 1 class tables, A 1500 W / P 100 W per t. 2.2.1, 20,0 kHz width —
was re-checked against the table and is correct.

**2. `bk-pro-1-19` — a second, worse defect the original checker did not catch.** The note
asserted that "ni jedan od dva dostupna izvora tu ovlast doslovno ne navodi" and that
confiscation "nije na tom popisu ni pod jednom stavkom". That is false. RK printed str. 231,
first sentence on the page, closing section 3.2.1 *Kaznene odredbe*, reads: **"Za gore navedene
prekršaje inspektor može izreći i zaštitnu mjeru oduzimanja radijske postaje."** The list of
offences it refers to (str. 230) includes causing harmful interference — "ako prigodom
stavljanja u pogon radijske opreme postupi suprotno odredbama članka 100. Zakona, tj. radijska
oprema uzrokuje štetne smetnje" — i.e. precisely this question's premise. So the HRS key's
answer c) *is* sourced; the previous note had only searched RK 3.2 (str. 229, the
inspection-powers bullet list), which genuinely omits confiscation, and generalised from that
one page to "no source at all". Rewritten: it now cites RK str. 230–231 for the confiscation
sentence, keeps str. 229 as the contrast (and explains why the near-identical `pro-091` in the
flat corpus answers *privremena zabrana* instead — that question offers it, this one does not),
and the ⚖ paragraph now makes the surviving, true point: RK 3.2.1 rests on the repealed Zakon o
telekomunikacijama NN 122/2003, NN150/22 čl. 24 regulates only the licensee's own duty (cease
without delay, clear within 30 days) and never the inspector's powers, and ZEK NN 76/22 is not
among the available sources so the measure's survival cannot be confirmed. `flag: check` kept.

**3. `bk-pro-1-15` — wrong RK section title.** The cite read "2.1.2. Privremena uporaba
amaterskih postaja u zemljama **izvan** CEPT-a, tiskana str. 213". The quoted sentence is on
str. 213, but it belongs to **2.1.2. Privremena upotreba amaterskih postaja u zemljama CEPT-a**
(heading on str. 212); the "izvan CEPT-a" section is 2.1.**3**, and its heading appears *after*
the quoted sentence. Section number was right, title was borrowed from the next section. Fixed
to name the section correctly with heading page and quote page separated. Same note also
claimed "svih **pet** jezičnih varijanti" — national language + German + English + French is
**four**; corrected, with a note that the same four-language rule covers the HAREC certificate
(verified on RK str. 213).

**4. `bk-pro-1-09` — an overstated quotation claim.** The note said RK str. 235 "prenosi
identičnu formulaciju" of NN150/22 čl. 15. st. 4. It does not: RK writes "Na prijedlog HRS-a,
Agencija može dodijeliti…" plus an extra clause about listeners only receiving, where the
Pravilnik writes "Agencija može, na prijedlog Saveza,…". Replaced with RK's actual sentence
quoted in full and an explanation that HRS and "Savez" are the same body per čl. 2. st. 1. t. 3.

**5. Header `_note` — wrong chapter page range.** It stated that RK chapter 3.3 "PROPISI I
UVJETI ZA DOZVOLE I RAD" spans printed str. 229–245. The heading actually stands on str. 231
(3.1 on 228–229, 3.2 with the penal provisions on 229–231). Corrected, and the CEPT chapters
2.1–2.2 (str. 212–213) added since four notes cite them.

**6. Five cites where the section heading sits on the page before the quoted line.** For
`bk-pro-1-04`, `-05`, `-08`, `-09`, `-11` the cite named a section heading and a page that were
one page apart (3.3.7 heading on 234 / quote on 235; 3.3.9 heading on 241 / list item 7 on 242;
3.3.11 heading on 242 / retention sentence on 243). Every quote was on the page cited, so
nothing was wrong, but the cites now state both pages so a reader who opens the heading page
does not conclude the citation is fictional.
