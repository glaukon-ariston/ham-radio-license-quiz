## New confirmed divergence found in this packet: bk-pro-5-08 (A-class PEP cap)

RK's old frequency-band table (3.3.8.1, str. 236) caps A-class transmitter PEP at 1000 W for
most bands, matching the HRS/exam answer (b, "1 000 W"). But NN150/22 Dodatak 1 t. 2.2.1 states
plainly: "razred A = 1500 W (61,8 dBm)" — the cap was raised to 1500 W under current law. This
is the same shape as the other flagged items (RK/HRS reflects an older numeric value than the
still-current NN150/22), but new to this packet — not previously documented for skupina V.
Flagged `check`; HRS answer left untouched (rule 1).

Of the 20, 6 carry `flag: "check"`:

- **bk-pro-5-07**: same pattern as `bk-pro-4-04` — RK's 14-year age threshold for the licence
  (3.3.1/3.3.4, str. 231-232) has no NN150/22 anchor (current Pravilnik's only age rule is
  parental consent under 18 for the exam application, čl. 9 st. 2).
- **bk-pro-5-08**: new finding, above (1000 W book vs. 1500 W NN150/22 Dodatak 1 t. 2.2.1).
- **bk-pro-5-12**: same pattern as `bk-pro-4-09`/`-14` — the 1000-10000 kn fine scale traces to
  the old Zakon o telekomunikacijama (RK 3.2.1); no ZEK article confirmed (ZEK not in `docs/`).
- **bk-pro-5-15**: same pattern as `bk-pro-4-13`/`bk-pro-3-15` — RK's "potvrda o tehničkoj
  ispravnosti" (samogradnja) is the same requirement as NN150/22 čl. 12 st. 3's "uvjerenje o
  tehničkoj sukladnosti", different term only.
- **bk-pro-5-19**: same pattern as `bk-pro-4-19`/`bk-pro-3-20` — RK's "5 godina" licence-validity
  text has no matching duration figure in current NN150/22 (only requires the licence to
  *state* a validity period, čl. 12 st. 11 t. 6).
