# P3-0019 — Book notes, skupina IV tehnički (b)

## Scope

Wrote `quiz/build/notes_book_s4_tehnicki_b.json`: one teaching note per id in
`quiz/build/work/packet_book_s4_tehnicki_b.json` (30 ids, `bk-teh-4-02` through `bk-teh-4-60`,
even numbers only — sibling of P3-0018's `notes_book_s4_tehnicki_a.json`, which covered the odd
half 01-59 from the same tiskana str. 321-331). Same `{note, formula?, cite}` shape as the other
`notes_book_*` files, no `links` key (matches convention — confirmed neither `link_check.py` nor
`check_notes.py` glob this filename). `packet_book_s4_tehnicki_b.json` (also in `files:`) was
read only, not edited, even though it and the live `book_parsed2.json` carry real defects — see
below.

## Method

1. Dumped the packet's 30 questions in full and rendered/dumped printed RK pages 321-331 with
   `booktool.py page RK N`, comparing every option letter and text word-for-word against the
   packet.
2. For every id, pulled and read the actual RK prose page(s) with `booktool.py page`/`find` and
   confirmed the specific claim/number/definition verbatim before citing it — not just a
   topically-plausible chapter. Two ids (`bk-teh-4-54`, `bk-teh-4-60`) have no book passage
   covering their specific scenario (RFI direction-finding technique; ground-rod material/depth)
   — cited only the exam page for those, per "cite nothing rather than a plausible page."
3. Multi-page/multi-citation entries (e.g. `bk-teh-4-10`, `bk-teh-4-18`) had each page
   individually opened and read, not inferred from one confirmed page in the group.
4. For the six ids with `has_figure: true` or flagged in `book_overrides.json`/
   `figs_book_index.json`, rendered the actual PNG crop (not just the OCR text) and read it
   directly — this is what surfaced the mislabelled `has_figure` defects and let me compute
   `bk-teh-4-46`'s oscilloscope reading and confirm `bk-teh-4-08`'s waveform identification
   against P3-0018's own `bk-teh-4-07` note (same figure set, explicitly cross-referenced).
5. Cross-checked all 30 answer letters against `book_keys.json` (skupina IV column) — all 30
   match; this is what let `bk-teh-4-40`'s defect be isolated as an option-text swap rather than
   a wrong-letter problem.

## One live content defect found, not fixed (out of this item's `files:` to edit as source)

**`bk-teh-4-40`** — the live `book_parsed2.json` (not just the work packet) has options **(c)
and (d) text swapped** relative to the printed page. Verified by rendering printed str. 327 at
200 dpi: real page is (a) 120 W, (b) 150 W, (c) 180 W, (d) 240 W. Live/packet currently holds
c="240 W", d="180" — reversed. Root cause visible directly in `book_parsed2.json`'s raw `opts`
coordinates: row 2 of the answer block has two entries at x≈71.76 (left column) and x≈288.24
(right column), but the left-column entry ("180") is labelled `d` and the right-column entry
("240 W") is labelled `c` — swapped left/right. `book_keys.json` (redak 40, stupac IV) reads
`D`, and on the real page that is "240 W", not "180" as the live/packet text under letter `d`
currently claims. Confirmed independently by the book's own worked passage (str. 87): Class-A
efficiency is "at best" 50% and "in reality" 40-45%; for 100 W output at ~42% that gives
≈240 W input, while 180 W would require ≈56% — above the book's own stated theoretical ceiling
for Class A, so 240 W is also the only physically consistent option. The note for
`bk-teh-4-40` is written around this verified real content (d = 240 W); the swap is not fixed in
`book_parsed2.json`/packet (out of this item's `files:`) — same disclosure pattern as P3-0018's
`bk-teh-4-55`/`bk-teh-4-47` handling.

## strict.py's `teh-273` disagreement belongs to this packet

P3-0018's report flagged that `strict.py`'s pre-existing `teh-273 <- bk-teh-4-52` disagreement
(HRS-A bank says "niskopropusni filtar", book says "pojasnopropusni filtar") "traces to
bk-teh-4-52, which is even — packet _b, not this item [P3-0018]", i.e. it belongs here. I did
verify `bk-teh-4-52` independently: `book_keys.json` redak 52 stupac IV = `B` =
"pojasnopropusni filtar", matching the book's own key and matching the reasoning (blocking both
subharmonics and harmonics needs a bandpass, not a low-pass). This is a **book-key-vs-HRS-bank**
disagreement between two separately-sourced questions with a similar stem, not something this
note-writing item touches or resolves (rule 1: never change an HRS answer). `strict.py`'s
count is unchanged at 4 disagreements before and after this item, so no regression — it was
already counted in the baseline.
