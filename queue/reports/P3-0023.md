## A false negative — the important find

`bk-teh-5-26` (MUF of sporadic E, answer "i do 220 MHz") asserted that **no** book section
numerically supports 220 MHz, and therefore cited only the exam page. **That assertion is
false.** Printed page **170**, section *7.1.3.2. Iznimni uvjeti rasprostiranja*, subsection
"Povremeno E-odbijanje", states verbatim:

> Maksimalna uporabljiva frekvencija, MUF za E_s, može dosegnuti do 150 MHz, ponekad i 220
> MHz, tako da se omogućuje komunikacija na 144 MHz na veće udaljenosti i s većim "preskokom".

Verified both in extracted text and by rendering the page region at 4× and reading it.

**Root cause, worth recording.** The original search was for the string `sporadič`. On printed
170 the book never spells the word — it uses only the symbol `E_s` (which the OCR further
mangles to `E,` and `£,`). A term search that looks correct can therefore miss the one page that
answers the question. The same page also gives the answer to the neighbouring question 27
(critical frequency for Es, up to 50 MHz). The note now cites str. 170, adds a `formula` block
separating f_c(Es) from MUF(Es) — the exact pair the question invites you to confuse — and
records the search trap so it is not repeated.

what the question names.

premošten kondenzatorom C₃ vrlo malog otpora za upotrijebljene frekvencije" (so the emitter
sits at AC ground) and that "za AF pojačala izaberu [se] kondenzatori većih kapaciteta, npr.
elektrolitički kondenzator... a kod RF pojačala ti kondenzatori su reda 1000 pF ili manji" —
i.e. the book's own reasoning for exactly this question, for all three capacitors including
C₃. The note was rewritten around this citation instead of the previous general reactance
