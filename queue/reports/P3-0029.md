## Method — full word-for-word verification, per the caller's brief

Given this series' documented history of citation defects, every claim in every note was
pulled and read directly from the source with `booktool.py` (RK, PZM, NN150/22 — the latter has
no printed folio, cited by članak/stavak/točka) before it went into a note — never reused from
the packet's own `current_cite` (answer-key table location only) or its `hrs_source` page
numbers. `questions/answer/options` for all 20 ids were compared word-for-word against the
printed dump of RK pages 373–375 and matched exactly.

## New finding: bk-pro-6-13's packet `question` field is OCR-truncated

Unlike prior propisi packets (P3-0025 found none in s5), this packet has one OCR defect: the
`question` text for `bk-pro-6-13` reads "Dozvola za amatersku radijsku postaju s upisanom
potvrdom o sigurnosti izdaje se" and stops there — the printed page 374 actually continues "...
ako postaja ima efektivnu izračenu snagu veću od:" before the options, with the OCR having
swallowed that clause around an embedded folio marker ("374") mid-sentence. Not edited (packet
is outside this item's write scope, treated read-only per the established convention for every
prior book-notes item); the note itself quotes the full, correct sentence and flags the
truncation explicitly so a reader isn't misled by the packet's shortened stem.

## Verified matches to current law (no flag) vs. confirmed divergence (flag: check)

Of the 20, 6 carry `flag: "check"`:

- **bk-pro-6-09** (14-year age for A-class licence): same pattern as `bk-pro-5-07`/`bk-pro-4-04`
  — RK 3.3.1/3.3.4 (str. 231–232) has no NN150/22 anchor (current Pravilnik's only age rule is
  parental consent <18 for the exam application, čl. 9 st. 2).
- **bk-pro-6-12** (klupska station notify-Agency threshold): RK 3.3.6 (str. 234) gives "4 tjedna"
  (28 days) with a mandatory report to Agency within 2 weeks; NN150/22 čl. 13 st. 2 gives a
  single, higher, unconditional 90-day threshold with no notify obligation and no separate
  klupska carve-out — new divergence, not previously documented (S4/S5 propisi didn't test this
  specific klupska rule).
- **bk-pro-6-14** (samogradnja): same pattern as `bk-pro-5-15`/`bk-pro-4-13` — RK's "potvrda o
  tehničkoj ispravnosti" is the same requirement as NN150/22 čl. 12 st. 3's "uvjerenje o
  tehničkoj sukladnosti", different term only.
- **bk-pro-6-15** (500–5000 kn fine for missing identification): traces to the old Zakon o
  telekomunikacijama (RK 3.2.1, "Ostale povrede") — unlike the S4/S5 packets' equivalent items,
  this one has a *direct, literal* RK match ("ako ne obavlja identifikaciju radijske postaje...
  Pravilnika, članak 15."), but the fine amount itself is not confirmed against the current ZEK
  (not in `docs/`), so flagged on the same basis as `bk-pro-5-12`/`bk-pro-4-09`/`-14`.
- **bk-pro-6-19** (5-year licence validity): same pattern as `bk-pro-5-19`/`bk-pro-4-19` — RK's
  "5 godina" licence-validity text has no matching duration figure in current NN150/22 (only
  requires the licence to *state* a validity period, čl. 12 st. 11 t. 6).

Direct, word-for-word confirmations against still-current NN150/22 (no flag): **-03**
(licence subject to "ostalim podzakonskim propisima", čl. 12 st. 1), **-04** (MM callsign suffix
for telephony from a vessel/ocean yacht, RK 3.3.7 str. 234 word-for-word including
"prekooceanskoj sportskoj brodici"; NN150/22 čl. 15 st. 2 t. 2 carries the same rule in shorter
phrasing — same shape as `bk-pro-5-13`), **-07** (licence right not extended to unlicensed
persons, NN150/22 čl. 3 st. 2 + čl. 23, RK 3.3.14 str. 244), **-10** (ITU Region 2 includes the
USA, RK 1.4 str. 212), **-11** (a member government may request ITU to ban international
amateur comms, RK 1.3 str. 210 — noted honestly that RK uses "vlada" and "administracija"
interchangeably in the same passage), **-13** (100 W ERP threshold for the "potvrda o
sigurnosti" requirement, RK 3.3.4.1 str. 233 word-for-word, matched by NN150/22 čl. 12 st. 2's
"jednaka ili veća od 100 W" — same numeric threshold old and new, only the referenced
subordinate regulation differs), **-16** (signals of unclear meaning banned, čl. 18 t. 7),
**-17** (must stay within permitted frequency band limits, NN150/22 čl. 22 st. 2 and RK 3.3.13
str. 243 — old and new agree word-for-word), **-18** (CEPT licence contains the CEPT class, čl.
12 st. 11 t. 4), **-20** (permitted receive scope includes time-standard signals, čl. 19 st. 1
and RK 3.3.10 str. 242).

**bk-pro-6-06** (J3F+F3F emission designators = narrowband television) is book-only: RK 3.3.8.2
(str. 239–241) tabulates J3F and F3F as each independently meaning "televizija (video)" under
different modulation schemes — no NN150/22 anchor exists for the ITU emission-designator table
itself, so only RK is cited, honestly, without implying regulatory confirmation.

**bk-pro-6-01**, **bk-pro-6-02**, **bk-pro-6-05**, **bk-pro-6-08** were solved by elimination or
left uncited rather than by a direct quote — same approach as P3-0025's bk-pro-5-05/-06:
- **-01** ("u svako pogodno vrijeme"): neither RK's inspection-powers chapter (3.2., str.
  229–230) nor NN150/22 čl. 22 st. 7 states any time restriction on inspection — reasoned by
  absence of restriction, not a literal quote of the phrase.
- **-02** ("postaju, dnevnik i dozvolu"): closest source is PZM's own drill question (str. 201,
  q11, "dozvolu za rad, postaju i dnevnik rada") — but PZM's own legend (str. 199) shows this
  question carries no Pravilnik article number, meaning even PZM's authors couldn't anchor it to
  a specific article; cited as supporting, not confirming.
- **-05** ("expired licence must be returned to the Agency"): searched RK, NN150/22, and PZM's
  appended full text of the *old* 2012 Pravilnik (str. 159–168) for "vratiti"/"istekla" — no
  hit outside the exam page itself in any of the three sources; cited nothing but the exam page,
  per the Avoid clause, rather than inventing a plausible article.
- **-08** ("no blank line in the log"): RK 3.3.11 (str. 242) never uses the words "prazni
  redak", but the other three options are each independently *required* by the same passage
  (UTC time, indelible ink, other station's callsign), leaving blank-line-prohibition as the
  only option consistent by elimination.
